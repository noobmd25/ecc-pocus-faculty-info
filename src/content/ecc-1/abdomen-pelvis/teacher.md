# Faculty Teaching Notes — Module 5: Abdomen & Pelvis (incl. Renal & Bladder)

This section is for instructors only and should not be released to students.

## Course-Delivery Reminders

- Use a flipped-classroom approach; maximize transducer time in the trainee's hands.¹
- Have them teach you the anatomy. If you have any doubts as to what you see, please keep them for when the proctor of the session passes by the station to answer the questions.
- Frequently rotate between students during the session for spaced repetition.¹

## Emphasize

- Emphasize FAST as a rule-in test for free fluid — a negative FAST does not exclude injury.²
- Emphasize outer-to-outer aortic measurement and the 3 cm threshold.¹²⁵

## Demonstrate

- Demonstrate aorta-vs-IVC differentiation in transverse before measuring.²
- Demonstrate how bladder-volume calculation is done in two planes, since the bladder is a 3-dimensional organ that is being evaluated by a 2-dimensional scan.⁴

## Reinforce

- Reinforce that all numeric thresholds have a reason for them to be that way (sensitivity and specificity) and they may change depending on the sources you read.
