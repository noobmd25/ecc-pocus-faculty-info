# Faculty Teaching Notes — Module 1: Foundations: Physics, Instrumentation & Knobology

This section is for instructors only and should not be released to students.

Combines "Introduction to Ultrasound" + "Instrumentation & Knobology" objectives.

## Course-Delivery Reminders

- Keep the learner-to-instructor ratio at ≤ 5:1 to ensure adequate skills training.¹
- Use a flipped-classroom approach; maximize transducer time in the trainee's hands.¹
- Frequently rotate between focused didactics and skills stations.¹

## Emphasize

- Emphasize the resolution-vs-penetration trade-off as the organizing concept for every probe choice.⁴
- Emphasize ALARA, TI, and MI — especially low output for ocular scanning later in the course.³⁴

## Demonstrate

- Demonstrate the orientation check (tap the probe marker, watch the screen) before every scan.⁴
- Demonstrate over-gaining a fluid-filled structure to show artifactual echoes, then correcting it.⁴

## Reinforce

- Reinforce that POCUS is a focused, binary, question-driven exam — not a complete consultative study.¹
- Reinforce structured troubleshooting: depth → gain → TGC → focus → frequency/preset.⁴

## Self-Assessment Answer Key

- **Q1:** Linear / high-frequency — best resolution for superficial structures.⁴
- **Q2:** Gain (then TGC) — over-gain produces false echoes in fluid.⁴
- **Q3:** Posterior shadowing deep to the stone; posterior enhancement deep to the bladder.¹
- **Q4:** ALARA = As Low As Reasonably Achievable; indices = Thermal Index (TI) and Mechanical Index (MI).³⁴
- **Q5:** Acceptable answers: obese body habitus, bowel gas, subcutaneous emphysema, operator inexperience → obtain CT/formal study.¹
